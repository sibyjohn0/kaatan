INGA → KAATAN

∞ Worlds Being Built

INGA isn't going away — she plays live. Intimate rooms, real audiences, folk-jazz-psychedelia that moves people from Goa to Delhi. The kind of artist that makes audiences sing back lyrics to songs they only just heard.

But INGA was always the inner world. KAATAN is the outer one.

A deliberate, intentional pivot into building a full universe: electronic, cinematic, a world with its own sonic architecture, visual language, and mythology. Already moving globally — a collaboration with UK producer Youngr, a live set in the Dubai Desert, music on shelves worldwide.

This isn't a brand new artist asking you to take a leap of faith. This is an established, award-winning artist constructing the next, larger world on top of a real foundation.
